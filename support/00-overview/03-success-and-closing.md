# 13. Definition of Success and Closing Principle

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Support and Operations Handbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the README at the root of this folder for the section map.

## 13.1 Definition of Success
**Business:** Critical journeys stay available, issues are resolved within agreed targets, and the business has confidence in the service.
**Technical:** Monitoring, backups, patching and recovery work and are tested; the estate stays on supported versions.
**Operational:** Processes, runbooks and ownership are clear; knowledge is not in one person's head.
**Engagement:** Reports are honest, improvements are delivered, and a clean renewal or handback is possible at any time.

## 13.2 Closing Principle

### Defined Service. Proven Recovery. Client-Owned Operations.

Support succeeds when the organisation can rely on its platform, understands how it is run, and could move to a new provider tomorrow without losing knowledge.
