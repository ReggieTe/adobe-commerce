# 3. Service Lifecycle Overview

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Support and Operations Handbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the README at the root of this folder for the section map.

| Phase | Name | Primary outcome | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Discovery and Assessment | Understand the estate, risks and expectations | S1 |
| 2 | Service Design | Support model, service levels, tools and processes agreed | S2 |
| 3 | Transition-In | Knowledge, access, runbooks and tooling ready; shadow period completed | S3 |
| 4 | Live Operations | Stable day-to-day run under defined processes | S4 |
| 5 | Service Management and Continual Improvement | Reporting, problem trends, capacity and improvements | S5 |
| 6 | Platform Lifecycle and Upgrade Management | Controlled response to Adobe releases, patches and end-of-support dates | S6 |
| 7 | Service Review, Renewal or Transition-Out | Reviewed service and a clean renewal or handback | S7 |

Phases 4–6 run continuously after S3, with periodic gate reviews. Reference diagram: Appendix C.1.
