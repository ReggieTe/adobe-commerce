# PHASE 7: SERVICE REVIEW, RENEWAL OR TRANSITION-OUT

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Support and Operations Handbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the README at the root of this folder for the section map.

## 10. Phase 7 – Service Review, Renewal or Transition-Out

### 10.1 Objective
Close each service period cleanly, either by renewing with improvements or by handing the service back or to a new provider.

### 10.2 Contract-Term Review
Assess performance against the SLA, cost, scope fit, risks and changes needed. Decide: renew, renegotiate scope, change provider, or insource.

**Deliverable:** Service Review Report.

### 10.3 Renewal
Update the service catalogue, SLA, staffing and tooling, with lessons from the term.

### 10.4 Transition-Out
If the provider or consultant is leaving:
1. Agree a transition plan and dates with the client and the incoming team.
2. Hand over the runbook library, knowledge base, ticket history, monitoring catalogue, access and credential inventory, vendor register and risk and debt registers.
3. Run a shadow and reverse-shadow period with the incoming team.
4. Transfer or revoke access and credentials through the vault; rotate secrets.
5. Confirm open incidents, problems and changes are assigned.
6. Obtain written acceptance of the handback.

**Deliverable:** Transition-Out Plan and Acceptance.

### 10.5 Consultant Exit and Service Ownership
**Hard exit condition.** The consultant cannot formally exit until the client has a **named service owner and provider** (internal team, MSP, partner or defined combination) who have accepted in writing responsibility for L1–L3 as applicable, coverage hours, monitoring, backup and DR operations, change and release management, vendor escalation, and custody of credentials and vendor accounts.

**Fallback if the client cannot appoint in time:** a time-boxed, separately contracted bridging service with a fixed end date, or a signed risk acceptance by the accountable executive and exit on a "handover with exceptions" basis. The consultant does not remain as default operator.

### 10.6 Phase 7 Exit Criteria (Gate S7)
**Entry condition:** named service owner and provider accepted (or fallback executed).
- Service review report accepted.
- Runbooks, knowledge base, registers and access inventory handed over.
- Shadow period completed.
- Credentials rotated or transferred.
- Open items assigned.
- Handback or renewal accepted.

**Final Deliverable:** Signed Service Handback or Renewal Acceptance.
