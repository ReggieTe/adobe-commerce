# PHASE 1: SERVICE DISCOVERY AND ASSESSMENT

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Support and Operations Handbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the README at the root of this folder for the section map.

## 4. Phase 1 – Service Discovery and Assessment

### 4.1 Objective
Understand what is to be supported, what state it is in, and what the business expects.

### 4.2 Business and Service Expectations
Critical business processes and revenue journeys, peak periods (campaigns, sales events), availability expectations, support hours, regional and language needs, regulatory and POPIA obligations, current pain points and past major incidents.

**Deliverable:** Business Service Requirements.

### 4.3 Estate Inventory
Products, versions and deployment models; environments; custom code and extensions; integrations and middleware; third-party systems and vendors; domains, certificates, DNS and CDN; user groups and admin roles; licences and contract dates; documentation status.

**Deliverable:** Service Estate Inventory (template A.8 for the component register).

### 4.4 Operational Health Review
Review monitoring coverage, alert quality, incident history, backup and restore evidence, DR tests, patch and upgrade status, access hygiene, secret and certificate expiry, runbook availability, known defects and technical debt.

**Deliverable:** Operational Health Assessment with risk ratings.

### 4.5 Vendor and Contract Review
Adobe support plan and entitlements, severity definitions and response targets, support portal access, named support contacts, third-party SLAs and escalation paths, renewal dates (verify per §1.5).

**Deliverable:** Vendor Support Register.

### 4.6 Skills and Capacity Assessment
Who currently supports what, skill gaps, key-person dependencies, working hours and on-call capacity.

### 4.7 Phase 1 Exit Criteria (Gate S1)
- Business expectations documented.
- Estate, vendors and contracts inventoried.
- Health assessment accepted and top risks owned.
- Skills and capacity gaps identified.
- Engagement model and named service owner confirmed in writing.

**Final Deliverable:** Service Discovery and Assessment Report.
