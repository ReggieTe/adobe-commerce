## 8. Phase 5 – Service Management and Continual Improvement

### 8.1 Objective
Use data to keep improving reliability, cost and user experience.

### 8.2 KPIs and Reporting
Typical measures: availability of critical journeys, incident counts by severity, response and resolution against target, repeat incidents, mean time to detect and restore, change success rate, backlog age, patch compliance, backup and restore success, user satisfaction, vendor ticket turnaround, cost per ticket. Publish a monthly service report (template A.9) and review it with the service owner.

### 8.3 Service Reviews
Monthly operational review, quarterly service review with the business, annual strategic review. Each produces owned actions.

### 8.4 Problem Trend Analysis
Group incidents by cause, component and change. Fund fixes for the top recurring causes.

### 8.5 Capacity and Performance Management
Track traffic, storage, queue and job volumes, response times and costs. Plan for campaigns and seasonal peaks. Feed findings to the development team.

### 8.6 Technical Debt and Risk Register
Keep a joint register with the development team. Rank by operational impact.

### 8.7 Cost and Licence Management
Track licence usage and renewal dates, and review whether capabilities in use justify their cost. This links to the downscale playbook when a reduction is considered.

### 8.8 Continual Improvement Register
Capture improvement ideas with benefit, effort, owner and status.

### 8.9 Phase 5 Review Criteria (Gate S5, reviewed periodically)
- Reports delivered on time and acted on.
- Top recurring problems have owned fixes.
- Capacity forecast reviewed.
- Improvement register maintained.
