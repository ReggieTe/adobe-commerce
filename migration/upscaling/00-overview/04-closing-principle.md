# 15. Closing Principle

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Migration Handbook* v1.1 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the root [README](../README.md) for the section map.

## Consultant-Led Transformation. Client-Owned Operations.

The consultant provides technical leadership, architecture, implementation guidance (or delivery, per the agreed model), migration expertise, stabilisation and knowledge transfer. The organisation retains ownership of its systems, data, infrastructure, Adobe subscriptions, vendor relationships and ongoing operations.

The consultant's final responsibility is not only a working platform, but a solution that the organisation or its appointed operational owner can understand, operate, maintain and continue scaling.
