# 14. Final Definition of Success

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Migration Handbook* v1.1 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the root [README](../README.md) for the section map.

**Business:** the platform supports agreed objectives; critical processes operate; customer and business experiences meet requirements; there is a path for growth.

**Technical:** target architecture implemented; Adobe products operate correctly; applications and integrations validated; data integrity maintained; security met; performance and scalability as agreed.

**Operational:** monitoring and alerting operate; backup and recovery documented and tested; support responsibilities assigned to a **named operational owner**; incident and escalation procedures exist; vendor relationships and ownership are clear.

**Consulting engagement:** deliverables accepted; documentation complete; knowledge transfer complete; client has accepted operational ownership; risks and technical debt documented; the consultant can exit without leaving the company dependent on undocumented knowledge or informal support.
