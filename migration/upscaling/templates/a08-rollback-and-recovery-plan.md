# A.8 Rollback and Recovery Plan

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Migration Handbook* v1.1 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the root [README](../README.md) for the section map.

- Decision authority and deputy.
- Triggers (data, functional, performance, security) with thresholds.
- Point-of-no-return time.
- Post-cutover transaction handling (capture, replay or reconcile).
- Traffic, DNS and integration reversal steps.
- Customer and stakeholder communication.
- Forward-recovery alternative.
