# PHASE 7: HYPERCARE, HANDOVER AND ADOBE DECOMMISSIONING

> Part of the *Adobe DXP Downscale Playbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the playbook's original numbering; see the root [README](../README.md) for the section map.

## 12. Phase 7 – Hypercare, Handover and Adobe Decommissioning

### 12.1 Hypercare
Monitor availability, checkout success, payments, orders, integrations, email delivery, search, errors and security. Fix migration defects, handle customer login and reset issues, and reconcile post-cutover orders. Keep an incident register. The operational owner works first-line with the consultant supporting.

**Deliverable:** Hypercare and Stabilisation Report.

### 12.2 SEO and Traffic Monitoring
Compare organic traffic, rankings, indexed pages, crawl errors, conversions and revenue with the Phase 1 baseline for an agreed period. Fix redirect gaps, soft 404s and duplicate content quickly. Report weekly during hypercare.

**Deliverable:** SEO and Traffic Recovery Report.

### 12.3 Support Model
Define L1–L3 and escalation to the target platform vendor, host, payment provider, app/plugin vendors and agencies.

**Deliverable:** Support and Escalation Matrix.

### 12.4 Documentation
Architecture, configuration, deployment, integrations, data mappings, migration and reconciliation procedures, archive access, security and access, backup and DR runbooks, troubleshooting. Credentials transfer only through approved secure mechanisms.

**Deliverable:** Documentation Repository.

### 12.5 Knowledge Transfer
Practical sessions and supervised exercises: platform administration, content and catalogue management, order handling, deployment, backup and restore, incident handling and archive retrieval. Keep training records.

**Deliverable:** Knowledge Transfer and Training Record.

### 12.6 Adobe Decommissioning
Run §18 after the fallback window closes and the business confirms stability. Decommissioning is a gated step with named owners and evidence.

### 12.7 Consultant Exit, Operational Ownership and Closure

**Hard exit condition.** The consultant cannot formally exit until:

1. The client has a **named, accountable operational owner** for the new platform (internal team, MSP, partner or defined combination) with written acceptance, coverage hours, L1–L3 and vendor escalation, backup and DR operation, change management, and custody of credentials and vendor accounts.
2. **Adobe termination actions are either complete or scheduled with named owners and dates** that fall within the contract timeline, and the client holds the evidence (§18).

**Timing.** The operational owner is appointed by D6.

**Fallback if the client cannot appoint in time.** Choose in writing between (a) a time-boxed, separately contracted bridging support period with a fixed end date, or (b) a signed risk acceptance by the accountable executive, with exit on a "handover with exceptions" basis. The consultant does not become the default operator.

**Closure package:** final implementation report; stabilisation report; SEO recovery report; outstanding issues and risk register; documentation repository; operational handbook; training records; vendor and escalation register; Adobe decommissioning evidence; future improvement roadmap; signed handover; client acceptance.

### 12.8 Phase 7 Exit Criteria (Gate D7)
**Entry condition:** operational owner named (or fallback executed) and Adobe termination plan evidenced.

- Platform stable for the agreed period; hypercare issues resolved or accepted.
- SEO and traffic within agreed tolerance, or remediation plan accepted.
- Documentation and knowledge transfer complete.
- Archive accessible and tested.
- Access and ownership transferred.
- Handover and closure accepted.

**Final Deliverable:** Signed Operational Handover, Adobe Decommissioning Evidence and Closure Report.
