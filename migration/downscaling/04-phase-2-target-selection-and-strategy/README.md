# PHASE 2: TARGET SELECTION AND STRATEGY

> Part of the *Adobe DXP Downscale Playbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the playbook's original numbering; see the root [README](../README.md) for the section map.

## 7. Phase 2 – Target Selection and Strategy

### 7.1 Objective
Decide where the organisation is going, what moves, what is archived or purged, and in what order.

### 7.2 Capability Rationalisation and Target Selection
Complete §4 and §3. Present the Capability Loss Register and the Target Platform Decision Record for client sign-off.

### 7.3 Application and Capability Disposition
For each capability and application decide: Retain, Replace, Rebuild, Reduce, Retire or Archive. Use the disposition matrix template from the up-scale handbook (A.2) with a Downscale column.

**Deliverable:** Disposition Matrix.

### 7.4 Data Strategy: Migrate, Archive or Purge
Decide per data class:

- **Migrate** to the target (active business use).
- **Archive** to cold storage with controlled access (retention only).
- **Purge** with approval and an audit trail (no legal or business basis to keep).

Define history depth for orders (for example, the last N years migrated, older archived), treatment of inactive customers, consent evidence handling and treatment of behavioural data.

**Deliverable:** Data Migration, Archive and Purge Strategy.

### 7.5 Content Strategy
Rank content by value (traffic, conversions, legal necessity). Decide rebuild, migrate as-is, consolidate or retire. Plan the content model for the target.

**Deliverable:** Content Strategy and Inventory Triage.

### 7.6 Integration Strategy
For each integration: re-point, replace connector, rebuild through middleware or retire. Decide the integration pattern for the target (native connectors, middleware, custom).

**Deliverable:** Integration Strategy.

### 7.7 Sequencing and Cutover Strategy
Options: single cutover; phased by channel, region or catalogue; parallel run with traffic split. Plan the **Adobe overlap period** (running both) to cover testing, cutover and a fallback window, and cost it against the contract timeline.

**Deliverable:** Roadmap and Cutover Strategy.

### 7.8 Risk, Cost and Resource Assessment
Seed the risk register with the downscale risks in Appendix A.6 (extraction limits, contract date, SEO loss, password loss, capability loss, target limits, payment gateway issues, retention breaches). Estimate resources and budget.

**Deliverable:** Risk Register, Resource Plan, Cost Assessment.

### 7.9 Operating Model Intent
Record the intended operator of the new platform (internal, MSP, partner) and the date by which the **operational owner** will be appointed. The latest date is D6.

### 7.10 Phase 2 Exit Criteria (Gate D2)
- Target Platform Decision Record signed.
- Capability Loss Register signed.
- Disposition, data, content, integration and sequencing strategies approved.
- Overlap period and budget approved.
- Operating model intent and appointment date recorded.

**Final Deliverable:** Approved Downscale Strategy and Roadmap.
