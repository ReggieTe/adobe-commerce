# PHASE 1: EXIT DISCOVERY AND ENTITLEMENT REVIEW

> Part of the *Adobe DXP Downscale Playbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the playbook's original numbering; see the root [README](../README.md) for the section map.

## 6. Phase 1 – Exit Discovery and Entitlement Review

### 6.1 Objective
Establish the real shape of the Adobe estate, what can be extracted and under what entitlement, what depends on it, and how much time the contract allows.

### 6.2 Contract and Licence Timeline
Complete §2.3. Produce a dated timeline of notice deadlines, term end dates, support windows and data-retention periods. Mark the **latest safe cutover date** and the **latest safe notice date**.

**Deliverable:** Adobe Contract and Entitlement Register and Exit Timeline.

### 6.3 Estate Inventory
For each Adobe product and environment record: version, deployment model, region, owner, environments, custom code and extensions, third-party extensions, users and admin roles, integrations, volumes, and operating costs.

**Deliverable:** Adobe Estate Inventory.

### 6.4 Data and Content Inventory
Use §2.4. For each data class: volume, quality, sensitivity, owner, retention requirement, extraction route, extraction entitlement and estimated extraction duration. Include orders by year, customers by activity, assets by size and rights, content by traffic and age.

**Deliverable:** Data and Content Inventory with Extraction Feasibility.

### 6.5 Integration and Tag Inventory
Document every integration: ERP, CRM, PIM, OMS, WMS, payments, tax, shipping, marketing, support, search, identity. Include Adobe-specific tags and scripts embedded in the site, webhooks, scheduled jobs, SFTP exchanges and IMS-based API credentials. Record owner, method, authentication, frequency, failure impact and disposition (keep, re-point, replace, retire).

**Deliverable:** Integration and Tag Register (see up-scale handbook template A.4 for columns).

### 6.6 Traffic, SEO and Revenue Baseline
Capture (before any change): organic traffic by landing page, top revenue pages, ranking keywords, indexed URL counts, backlinks to key URLs, page speed, conversion rate, revenue by channel, search console data and sitemaps. This is the benchmark for post-launch comparison.

**Deliverable:** Traffic, SEO and Revenue Baseline.

### 6.7 Capability Usage Analysis
Establish which Adobe features are actually used (not merely licensed). Interview users, review admin activity and reports. Feed this into §4.

**Deliverable:** Capability Usage Report.

### 6.8 Security and Compliance Discovery
Review personal-information holdings, consent evidence, residency obligations, financial-record retention rules, audit logs, privileged accounts and credentials to be revoked later. Identify which records must be kept for tax, accounting or legal reasons, and for how long, and confirm with counsel and the finance owner.

**Deliverable:** Security, Compliance and Retention Report.

### 6.9 Phase 1 Exit Criteria (Gate D1)
- Contract timeline established and latest safe dates agreed.
- Estate, data, integrations and tags inventoried.
- Extraction entitlement and feasibility confirmed for every data class.
- Traffic and SEO baseline captured.
- Retention and compliance requirements recorded.
- Engagement model and any build partner confirmed in writing.
- Driver for the downscale recorded and agreed.

**Final Deliverable:** Exit Discovery and Current-State Report.
