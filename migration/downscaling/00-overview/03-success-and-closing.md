# 20. Definition of Success and Closing Principle

> Part of the *Adobe DXP Downscale Playbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the playbook's original numbering; see the root [README](../README.md) for the section map.

## 20.1 Definition of Success
**Business:** Retained capabilities work; agreed losses are accepted; revenue and traffic recover to the agreed tolerance; costs meet the business case.

**Technical:** Data is migrated and reconciled; integrations and payments work; performance and security meet requirements; archive is accessible.

**Operational:** A named operational owner runs the platform; monitoring, backup and DR work; documentation and knowledge transfer are complete.

**Exit:** Adobe is cleanly decommissioned with evidence; no orphaned credentials, tags or data remain; contracts are terminated on time.

**Engagement:** Deliverables accepted; risks and technical debt documented; the client is not dependent on undocumented knowledge.

## 20.2 Closing Principle

### Deliberate Exit. Verified Data. Client-Owned Operations.

A downscale succeeds when the organisation leaves Adobe on its own timetable, keeps what matters, knowingly gives up what it chose to, and runs the new platform with confidence.
