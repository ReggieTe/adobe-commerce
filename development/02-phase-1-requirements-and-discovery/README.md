# PHASE 1: REQUIREMENTS AND TECHNICAL DISCOVERY

> Part of the *Enterprise Adobe DXP Development Handbook* v1.0 (Review Draft). Section numbers (§) refer to the handbook's original numbering; see the README at the root of this folder for the section map.

## 4. Phase 1 – Requirements and Technical Discovery

### 4.1 Objective
Turn business needs into a prioritised, testable backlog, and understand the technical landscape the solution must fit.

### 4.2 Business and Stakeholder Requirements
Identify stakeholders, business goals, users and journeys, success measures and constraints (budget, dates, regulation, brand, accessibility). Capture requirements as user stories or equivalent with acceptance criteria.

**Deliverable:** Requirements Backlog with Acceptance Criteria.

### 4.3 Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)
Define measurable targets for: performance (page and API response times, throughput), availability, scalability, security, privacy and POPIA obligations, accessibility, browser and device support, SEO, observability, maintainability and localisation.

**Deliverable:** NFR Register with measurable targets.

### 4.4 Current-State Technical Review
Review existing code, configuration, content models, integrations, environments, pipelines, test coverage, known defects and technical debt. Identify custom code that duplicates product features.

**Deliverable:** Technical Landscape and Debt Report.

### 4.5 Product Fit and Build-vs-Configure Analysis
For each requirement decide: configure, extend with a supported mechanism, integrate an external service, or build custom. Record why.

**Deliverable:** Fit-Gap and Build/Configure Decision List.

### 4.6 Dependencies, Risks and Assumptions
Third-party systems, vendor lead times, data availability, licence limits, team skills, release windows. Seed the risk register.

### 4.7 Phase 1 Exit Criteria (Gate E1)
- Backlog prioritised with acceptance criteria.
- NFR targets measurable and agreed.
- Current-state and debt report reviewed.
- Build/configure decisions recorded.
- Engagement model and build partner confirmed in writing.

**Final Deliverable:** Approved Requirements and Technical Discovery Pack.
